Privacy policy

Personal rclone backup is a private app used only by its owner, to back up their own files to their own Google Drive accounts with rclone.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, only to keep a backup copy of the owner's files there.

What it collects and shares

  • It collects no personal data and has no server: everything runs on the owner's own computer, which talks to Google Drive directly.
  • The Google login it receives stays on the owner's computer, in rclone's own settings file.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with or shown to anyone else. There's no tracking, advertising or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
